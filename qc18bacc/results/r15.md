## 1. Mechanism check (PI hypothesis: idle H capacity over the S macros) [S]
S / B / params / IMG macros: LEF OBS on M1-M3 only (S: M2 652, M3 1165 rects; signal pins M1 / M3 on the macro south edge, y 13 um).
`lay15.py` (route guides, x 860-3200 = columns 0-5, demand / nominal tracks, no derate; full tables in results/r15/):

|---|---|---|---|---|
| b450 (band above S rows) | 20.7 / 12.6 / 5.1 % (S0-S2 allH 2-5 %) | 88.8 / 74.5 / 50.9 % | 11-19 % | 28-42 % |
| mid (band between S1 / S2) | 38.0 / 24.9 / 11.3 % | 75.3 / 63.3 / 42.8 % | 18-31 % | 32-45 % |
**Confirmed:** with the band above the 4 S rows the router leaves M5-M9 over S rows 0-2 almost empty (allH 2-5 %) while the band runs at
90 % nominal (M3 110 %): crossings at any vertical cut use only 28-42 % of the die-height H tracks.  The deficit is local (band + S3).
Moving the band between S rows 1 and 2 doubles the H capacity adjacent to the logic (S1 below, S2 above) and the over-S-row share
of the crossings rises to 18-31 %.  H overflow (3 iterations, adj 0.25): **280.6 k -> 20.6 k tracks (-93 %)**.

## 3. Why each variant behaves as it does [S / R]
* **split fails (3.85 % with B):** the top half band at the north edge still hosts the S-read path to the 2048 act-port pins s_q (fixed
  at the north edge of the west strip, x 24-843).  H overflow records over S3 / top band: S Q 61 % / 26 %, s-Q>act-out 27 % / 18 %
  of the net-tracks: all 32 S macros' Q funnel west along the north edge into the NW corner.
* **mid moves that traffic:** the S-read glue sits in the middle band, runs west in it and climbs the west strip (no S macros there)
  to the s_q pins; H overflow -93 %.  It costs vertical traffic over S rows 2 / 3 from the north-edge column pins: V overflow over S2
  = prm 40 %, glue:reg>col-out 16 %, PORT wb_fy 15 %, S3 pins 10 % of the net-tracks (S pins are on the macro south edge, so S3's
  nets cross S2), 78 k V tracks at adj 0.25.  Placed std +42 k um2 (+2.2 %) and HPWL 40.3 -> 48.6 m (+21 %): the north-pin nets
  are ~450 um longer.
* **B (M5-M9 0.10):** GRT derated capacity M5 8.53 -> 10.08 M, M6 8.50 -> 10.05 M, M7 4.23 -> 4.87 M (+15-18 %); M8 / M9 unchanged
  (their reduction is PDN, not the adjustment).  On mid it removes 88 % of the V overflow over S2 (M6 is the free V layer over the macros:
  M2 OBS, M4 74 %).  On b450 alone it leaves 5.01 % (126.7 k H tracks in the band): **B without the floorplan change does not close it.**
  Justification: M5+ carry no std-cell pins and no macro pins (S / B / P pins M1 / M3); the 0.25 reservation is for pin access.  Routed
  R5a column (results/r5a.md, 9 039 final markers): M2 shorts 4 711, M3 1 224, VIA1 1 026, M2 spacing 955, **M4-M7 < 700 together**;
  R13 (R9a act block): 1.03 M of the M2 shorts follow signal-pin density.  DR markers by layer on this run: section 4.
* **C (0.80 / 0.80) vs 0.86 / 0.80:** -0.4 pt (6.87 -> 6.51 %), -0.08 pt with B (1.07 -> 0.99 %): second-order once the band has the capacity.

## Mechanism / conclusion
The hub's routing failure at the band-450 die was a **local** H shortage: all std logic in one band above 4 macro rows whose M4-M9
were idle (allH 2-5 % over S rows 0-2), with the S-read traffic of all 32 macros funnelling west to the act-port pins in the NW
corner.  Putting the logic band between S rows 1 and 2 (same die, same macros, `QB15_FP=mid`) uses the H tracks over the
macros on both sides of the band and lets the S-read path climb the west strip: H overflow -93 % at equal settings.  The cost is
vertical: north-edge column-pin nets (prm / reg>col-out / f_y) and S3's south-edge pins now cross S rows 2-3, where only M4 / M6 / M8
are free.  The upper-layer adjustment (B) supplies that (and the remaining H): 1.07 % / 0.99 % gcells at 3 iterations,
**0.06 % (mid, 15 it.) / 0.14 % (midc, 10 it.)** with more iterations.  Neither lever alone suffices (b450 + B 5.01 %, mid alone 6.87 %).
Die stays 3990 x 1751 = 6.986 mm2; placed std +2.2 % (1.936 vs 1.894 mm2), HPWL +21 %; GRT-parasitic WNS (ideal clock, no repair)
-2.13 to -2.21 ns vs -2.70 / -2.96 for b450 + B / b450 at 1.85 ns.
