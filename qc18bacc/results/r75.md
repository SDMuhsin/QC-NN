## Root cause: why ICC2 leaves a clock-gate output over max_cap [M] (ICC2 probes on the r74 labels, diag_r74 p1-p6, x1-x4)

Checked and ruled out:
- CTS cell list: CKLNQD1-16 carry the cts purpose; the CTS "ICG reference list" has CKLNQD1/2/3/4/6/8/12/16 (route.log).
- Larger ICGs excluded: no. CTS resized the gates: place label 227 x CKLNQD1 (size_only true, DC); cts label 14 D1, 138 D2,
  38 D3, 31 D4, 5 D6, 1 D12 (size_only false, application_fixed, not dont_touch).
- ss max_cap ignored by CTS: no. CTS-101 lists all 9 scenarios; synthesize_clock_trees -postroute prints a per-corner QoR
  and counts NetsWithDRC = 1, cap cost 0.0006, for this one net at ss. report_clock_settings: 0.5 ns / 0.6 pF default,
  so the library limit (0.1185 pF) is the binding one.
- Clock nets dont_touch / size_only after CTS: no.
- Clock-gate enable timing: E setup slack 3.34 ns (wcl), hold 0.285 ns (ff). A manual size_cell to CKLNQD3 is legal in
  place (4.2 vs 3.8 um, check_legality clean).

The chain:
1. CTS sizes the clock gates on its own estimate. cts.compile.enable_global_route defaults to false, so the virtual
   router is used, and area recovery stops each gate at the smallest size that passes.
2. clock_opt then detail-routes the clock (cts label: 439 detail-routed nets). The routed net is 0.55 fF over:
   clk_gate_shd_reg_3/latch/Q at 0.11905 vs 0.11850 pF, ss only. It is the only clock DRC in any corner at the cts label
   (report_clock_qor -type drc_violators); it stays over through route and eco (route_drv.rpt, eco_drv.rpt, PT -0.00).
3. ICC2's post-route clock DRC fixing does not resize clock gates:
   - synthesize_clock_trees -postroute -routed_clock_stage detail: "Drivers Attempted 1, Failed subgraph evaluation 1,
     NumCTCells changed 0" (its later network-flow stage: 22 attempted, 4 commits, 0 cells changed).
   - Also failed with CKLNQD alternatives declared (user_function_class; get_alternative_lib_cells then lists
     CKLNQD1-24, empty by default because function_id is empty).
   - Also failed with cts.compile.size_pre_existing_cell_to_cts_references true and cts.common.verbose 3, which gave no
     reason.
   - route_opt's CCD clock DRC fixer (r74 second route_opt): "Drivers Attempted 0".
   - PrimeTime fix_eco_drc never touches clock nets.
   So a clock-gate output that CTS sizes at zero slack and that detail routing then pushes over can never be repaired.
- R65 premise: "no alternative lib cells" is literally true (empty function_id) but is not the cause. Declaring
  alternatives changes nothing, and CTS sizes the gates anyway. The rest of R65's mechanism holds: zero-slack sizing on
  an estimate, then routed parasitics over the limit, then no post-route repair.
- The r75 cts step tested the remaining CTS-stage lever [M]. It ran with cts.compile.enable_global_route true, so CTS
  used the global router and not the virtual router ("Global route buffer aware mode is ON").
  - CTS QoR for ss: NetsWithDRC 1 -> 0 at "Post Initial DRC Fixing", and still 0 at "Post Final DRC Fixing".
  - After clock_opt's detail clock routing: the same gate (clk_gate_shd_reg_3) is over the ss cap again, and it is the
    only clock DRC in the 9 scenarios (summary "cts clock_drc").
  - Clock repeaters 220 (r74 211); clock cell area 3114 um2 (r74 2962).
  - So no CTS estimate removes the routing delta, and the option was dropped from cts.tcl. The r75 cts label is the
    one exception: it was built with the option on.
- The ICG's upstream net does not block a resize [M]. It is a dedicated CKBD12 (cto_buf_9810, fanout 1), and CKLNQD2 /
  CKLNQD3 CP pin caps are 1.495 / 1.499 fF.

## Fix (R75)

The repair belongs at the sign-off ECO, because no ICC2 step can make it.
- PrimeTime fix_eco_drc -cell_type clock_network (documented: "sizing or inserting cells in the clock network"),
  max_transition and max_capacitance, without physical constraints. The buffers are the CTS buffers (site/pnr.tcl
  QC_PNR_CTS_BUF that the dont_use list allows: CKBD4 / 8 / 12 / 16). ICC2 eco.tcl then places and legalises them
  (place_eco_cells), as it does for the data leftovers.
- Code: signoff/pt/drv_eco.tcl, qb_eco_clock_buffers and qb_eco_drc <pmode> <bufs> <cbufs>.
- Probe [M], r74 final layout, PT ss: before, latch/Q 0.12048 / 0.1185 pF. fix_eco_drc clock_network inserted 1 CKBD4
  (3.24 um2). After: remaining violations 0, unfixable 0. PT gives no sizing option, because PT also has no CKLNQD
  alternatives.
- Not a margin and not a waiver: the target is the library limit, and the fix is a PT ECO command on the sign-off
  timing.

R65's settings:
- route.tcl: the comment "no alternative lib cells" was false as the cause, and it is replaced.
- route_opt.flow.enable_ccd_clock_drc_fixing always_on is kept. Signal routing changes clock-net coupling after CTS,
  and the fixer repairs clock buffers (R65 [M]: 1 attempted, 1 committed). It does not touch clock gates.
- place_opt.flow.merge_clock_gates false is kept, with numbers (place.tcl):
  - Merged 160-sink gates: 4 over the limit at the r64 cts label, by up to 15 fF.
  - DC's 32-sink gates: 1 over, by 0.55 fF (r74, r75).
  - Each such gate now costs one ECO clock buffer.
  - Cost of keeping the gates unmerged: +1138 um2 (0.10 % of the core).
- The deprecated route_opt.flow.xtalk_reduction (NDMUI-443) is removed from icc2/common.tcl qb_route_setup.
- PT parasitics logs: each qb_pt run sets PT's parasitics_log_file to <log>_parasitics.log through QB_PT_PARA_LOG, which
  signoff/pt/common.tcl qb_pt_spef reads. PT runs sharing one directory no longer share ./parasitics_command.log; the
  chip top runs its corners in parallel in one directory, so it had the same collision. ETMs and views come only from
  run.sh post and views.

## Open

1. route_opt's CCD clock DRC fixer attempted 0 drivers in r74 and r75, although ICC2's own route_drv.rpt lists clock
   pins over max_cap (r75: a plain CKND inverter). Why it is idle is not known. R65 saw 1 commit. The setting is kept,
   but it is not effective in this design; the PT clock-network pass does the fixing.
2. ICC2 does not resize a clock gate after routing ("Failed subgraph evaluation"), and the tool gives no reason, even at
   cts.common.verbose 3. A clock gate that routing pushes over gets PT clock-network buffering (CKBD after the gate),
   which is tested only on r74 ss (probe). r75 had no gate over at the route label.
3. Round 1 predicted != actual (5 data-path violators). The R73-type route_eco side effect is not fully gone.
4. merge_clock_gates on was not re-measured with the new ECO pass. Its necessity rests on R65 r64 numbers plus the R75
   fact that no ICC2 stage resizes gates.
