Measurement only, on the R16 route input `r16_se` (db/cts_rt.odb, CTS + post-CTS repair), tau3.  No RTL / flow change.
Labels: [M] measured by my scripts on tool output, [S] tool-reported, [E] estimate / model, [R] recommendation.

* Each lb slot (2 048 b, both ctx) spans the whole height: all 16 (ctx, slot) centroids at x 358-379, y 1039-1048.  So every S1
  row's lbm inputs come from y 260-1875, and the placer puts **every row's S1 tree at the middle**: S1-comb y median per act row
  924-1419 (sd 170-340 um) while the act rows are at y 163 ... 1880 (row 0: act y 163, S1 tree y 924).  All 16 trees overlap in
  y 900-1300 = the overflow band; the S1 band is narrow in x (225-663), lb / pre-mux use ~670 of 900 um.

## 4. DR marker map (r16_d iteration 0) vs 0.25 overflow map [M]

* Coincide in y: markers peak y 900-1300 (y 800-1300 holds 61 % of the 1.36 M markers on 25 % of the height); overflow is
  100 % there.  100-um bins: Pearson 0.88 (0.87 vs M2+M3 shorts), Spearman 0.60; the top 20 % marker bins hold 86 % of the
  overflow.  At 0.15: Pearson 0.68 (band too narrow).
* Differ in x and outside the band: V overflow is flat over x 0-900, markers peak x 200-700 (S1 comb / lb pin density);
  ~39 % of markers lie in y < 800 or > 1300 with zero overflow (local M2 pin access, R13 mechanism).

**Conclusion:** the 0.05 clean GRT was an artefact; the DR failure is a real V bisection overflow (115 % at 0.25) caused by S1
(tree 41 % + lbm 27 % of the worst cut), with the lb write fan (~17 %) second; select broadcasts are irrelevant.
[R] Localise S1 per act row (row-local lb words / S1 inputs) or move the S1 trees off one band; derating alone cannot fix 85 % raw-track demand.
