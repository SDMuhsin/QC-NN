## 5. Timing diagnosis (for the PI's investigation) [M unless marked]
- fyi -> s4 (pcol): a 12-stage FA1D0 ripple chain (DC intadd_104), all D0 and never upsized: 2.1 ns tt / 6.7 ns ss.
  This is an RTL / synthesis limiter, not routing. It sets ss reg2reg in both arms.
- **Constraint bug: QB_IO_LAT 1.30 is stale.** Real clock-pin latency, A tt, 74 884 pins: p10 / p50 / p90 / max = 1.564 /
  1.697 / 1.797 / 2.956 ns (the 1.30 was measured before the SPEF fix annotated the clock nets). The 0.4 ns gap costs every
  reg2out path 0.4 ns. In B tt, 13.5k of 13.7k hold violators are in2reg (input min delay 0.05 P + L): the same gap.
  Reg2reg hold: 403 endpoints, mostly -0.05 to -0.10. The clk root is reached through 4 wire repeaters (0.66 ns).
  The 7 clock pins above 2.5 ns are the late leaf of the ICG g_wbc_hdo (BUFFD2 slew 0.85, +0.97 ns).
- Tried QB_IO_LAT 1.70 in P&R (r31_A2, tau5): OR's pre-route latency estimate is lower, so output hold (min delay -L)
  flagged 24k endpoints, and post-CTS hold repair hit RSZ-0060 (max buffer count) and crashed. Deleted. The shift has to be
  measured where it is used: a PT-only L = p50 (not run, stopped at the PI's request).
