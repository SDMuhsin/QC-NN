# R33: hub timing slowness, unbiased bug hunt (second pass)
Labels: [M] measured, [S] single run, [E] estimate. Runs analysed: r32_LB (B arm, 2.80 ns, QB_IO_LAT 1.39, routed, PT
tt reg2reg -1.027 / 261.3 MHz) and its parent r31_B (same netlist / floorplan / placement / cts.odb). tau6 only (tau4 was
down). Nothing in the repo changed except this file. No default changed, nothing committed. Tau times are UTC-6.
Scripts: tau6 `/tmp/ckp908/qc18bacc/r33/` (sec. 8).

## 0. Summary
Three own-side defects, each measured on its own:

| # | defect | where | what it costs (B arm, tt) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No timing repair after global routing.** reg2reg is repaired at placement parasitics only, where it is already clean. All of the reg2reg loss appears at global routing, and nothing repairs it | `impl/r21/post_grt.tcl` (the hub's POST_GRT_TCL: antenna + reports only) | 13 internal violating endpoints at placement parasitics, **928 at GRT parasitics** (b2w_q -0.288 -> **-0.960**, PT routed -1.027). A post-GRT `repair_timing` (240 s, +154 um2) takes GRT reg2reg **-0.960 -> -0.387** |
| 2 | **The place step's incremental global placements collapse the repeater chains that repair_design has just built.** The next repair_design then re-buffers the gaps and never removes the clumps | `impl/pnr/gpl_incr.tcl` (QB_GPL_INCR 1) and `impl/r9b/place_pre.tcl` QB9_GPL2 1 | +23k buffers / +226k um2 in the place step. At cts_rt, 17k of them (146k um2, 7 % of std area) are useless. The 300 worst routed reg2reg paths carry on average 9.7 clumped repeater stages (0.37 ns). Collapsing them: GRT reg2reg -0.960 -> -0.871, WNS -1.652 -> -1.564 |
| 3 | **Clock tree: repair_clock_nets inserts data buffers (BUFFD16 / BUFFD2) into clock nets**, and the g_wbc gated subtree ends up at +0.9 to +1.2 ns latency | `flow/tsmc65/pnr/or/cts.tcl` (repair_clock_nets right after CTS, before any parasitics estimate) | 17 data buffers on clock nets in cts.odb (3 BUFFD2). 37 flops have 2.0-2.79 ns latency (p50 1.61). They set the tt all-path limiter: -1.684, and -1.349 without them |

End to end (sec. 6): fixes 1 and 2 applied to r32_LB's cts_rt, then routed (DR 0) and signed off in PT. **tt reg2reg -1.027 ->
-0.518 (261.3 -> 301.4 MHz; 1069 -> 79 violating endpoints), ss reg2reg -2.825 -> -1.840 (177.8 -> 215.5 MHz)**, with
146k um2 less std area. All-path is unchanged (219 vs 223 MHz tt), because defect 3 and the IO budget set it.

What the reg2reg limiter is made of (b2w_q pointer loop, PT routed -1.027) [M]: about 0.67 ns is lost at global routing,
mostly long nets taking 1.4-1.9x their HPWL on thin metal (sec. 1). Another 0.33 ns is the delay of 8 clumped repeater stages (sec. 2). Removing them recovers 0.09 ns at GRT, since the
surviving repeater takes over part of the load.
The logic itself is about 1.6 ns of the 3.6 ns data path.

## 1. Defect 1: timing is never repaired at global-route parasitics [M]
Same db (r32_LB `db/cts_rt.odb`, the production post-CTS-repaired placement). IO budget as production (L 1.39). OR, tt.
| state | all WNS / viol. endp. | internal endpoints violating (IO false-pathed) | reg2reg WNS (b2w_q -> b2w_q) |
|---|---|---|---|
| cts.odb (r31_B), placement parasitics, before the post-CTS repair | -1.880 / 3180 | **13** (worst -0.448; repair_timing fixes them in 5 s -> -0.026) | |
| cts_rt.odb, placement parasitics (what the flow optimises against) | -1.006 / 3049 | | **-0.288** |
| same db, `global_route` (production recipe) + `estimate_parasitics -global_routing` | -1.652 / 4085 | **928** | **-0.960** |
| PT routed sign-off of the same db (r32_LB, R32) | | | -1.027 |
| **+ post-GRT `repair_timing -setup` (IO false-pathed, repair_tns 100, 240 s) + DPL + incremental GRT** | -1.652 / 3233 | | **-0.387** (+154 um2) |
- OR's GRT estimate matches PT routed (-0.960 vs -1.027) [M], so the loss happens at global routing, not in DR or in the SPEF.
- Mechanism on the limiter path (b2w_q_reg_2 -> ... -> b2w_q_reg_0). The data path crosses the band twice (x 524 -> 2392 ->
  386 -> 2365 um at y ~ 1270) through 7 long repeater nets. Placement estimate vs routed, per net (cap pF / wire delay ns):
  net248740 0.175 / 0.072 -> 0.276 / 0.181; u_top_n47186 0.111 / 0.036 -> 0.200 / 0.123; net233004 0.130 / 0.040 -> 0.203 / 0.111;
  net242156 0.162 / 0.062 -> 0.243 / 0.150; net242155 0.164 / 0.063 -> 0.250 / 0.152 (`r33_r2r_tt.rpt`, `pr33_LB_ctsrt.rpt`).
  Routed length vs pin HPWL: net248740 1107 vs 722 um (361 um of it vertical M6 between two pins 4 um apart in y),
  u_top_n47186 847 vs 473, net233004 888 vs 562, net242155 1066 vs 720 (`np33_LB.txt`, `wl33_LB.txt`). The route dips
  about 120-180 um south of the y ~ 1270 band corridor and comes back. These are congestion detours. Design-wide, 2-pin nets
  route at 1.00-1.07x HPWL (p90 1.08-1.54, `dt33_LB.txt`), so the detours sit on the band nets, where the critical paths are.
- The post-CTS repair cannot see this: at placement parasitics only 13 internal endpoints violate. The flow's
  `route.tcl` provides POST_GRT_TCL "(e.g. timing repair at global-route parasitics)", but the hub's hook
  (`impl/r21/post_grt.tcl` -> `impl/r9b/post_grt.tcl`) only repairs antennas and writes reports.
- Ruled out as the cause: QB_TNS 10. On the same cts.odb, repair_tns 10 vs 100 gives legal WNS -1.006 vs -0.982 and
  3008 vs 2835 violating endpoints (1664 s vs 3069 s). Of the violators, 2840 are IO (output ports), so the 10 % cut
  only ever selects IO endpoints. That does not matter, because internal paths are clean at that point anyway.

## 2. Defect 2: incremental global placement collapses repair_design's repeater chains [M]
Place step replayed exactly (production env, `pl33.sh`, run r33_pl from r31_B's pdn.odb), with a census after every
global_placement / repair_design / DPL (`ins33.tcl`). A "pair" is a buffer whose output net has one load, a buffer.
| place-step stage | buffers (um2) | buffer -> buffer nets: p10 / p50 distance | adjacent pairs (< 10 um) | OR WNS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gpl (timing-driven) | 365 (1.5k) | | | (unbuffered) |
| repair_design 1 | 38 870 (402k) | 13 948: 391 / **719 um** | **9** | **-0.878** |
| incremental gpl (gpl_incr.tcl, density 0.65) | 38 870 | 13 948: 3.0 / **17.8 um** | **6 370** | -1.937 |
| repair_design 2 | 51 571 (526k) | 24 984: 3.9 / 471 um | 6 398 | -0.908 |
| incremental gpl (QB9_GPL2, density 0.65) | 51 571 | 24 984: 2.4 / 7.1 um | 14 546 | -2.192 |
| repair_design 3 | 61 991 (628k) | 34 778: 2.8 / 71 um | 14 561 | -1.124 |
| DPL (= db/place.odb) | 61 991 (628k) | 34 778: 3.0 / 72 um | **14 240** | **-1.131** (production r31_B: -1.077, 14 385 pairs) |
- The repeater chain that repair_design spaces at ~720 um is pulled together by the next incremental global placement
  (buffers are 2-pin cells, so the wirelength model gains nothing by keeping them spread). Repair then re-buffers
  the now-long gaps (+12.7k, then +10.4k buffers) and never removes the clumped ones. Net result of the two rounds:
  +23k buffers, +226k um2, WNS -0.878 -> -1.131.
- In the routed design (PT tt, r32_LB, `pp33e.tcl`): the 300 worst reg2reg paths carry on average 9.7 clumped
  repeater stages (a BUFF / CKBD driving one buffer on a net < 12 fF), 0.37 ns per path. The limiter
  b2w_q_reg_2 -> b2w_q_reg_0 has 8 of them, 0.325 ns. Example: `wire223847 -> wire223846` (0.027 ns each, 6 fF), and
  `wire190767 -> wire190766 -> wire229332 -> wire229331` (4 buffers back to back, 6-7 fF loads).
- A/B on r32_LB cts_rt (`coll33.tcl`): remove the upstream buffer of every adjacent pair (16 998 buffers, 146k um2,
  std area 2.085 -> 1.938 mm2). Placement reg2reg -0.288 -> -0.147. **GRT reg2reg -0.960 -> -0.871, WNS -1.652 ->
  -1.564, violators 4085 -> 3520.**
- Why the incremental gpl is there (impl/r5b note: without it DPL "sat > 30 min in diamond search" on repair buffers
  over macros). Still true: the place step with QB_GPL_INCR 0 QB9_GPL2 0 (r33_p1) reached the same repair_design-1
  state (38 929 buffers, 9 adjacent pairs, WNS -0.873), then DPL ran > 20 min and I killed it. So the fix cannot be
  "drop the incremental gpl". Either collapse the clumps after it (as the A/B above), or `remove_buffers` after the last
  incremental gpl and run one repair_design. On r31_B place.odb that variant (`sb33.tcl` rebuf) gives 36 228 buffers
  (375k um2, 19 adjacent pairs) and DPL finishes in minutes. reg2reg -0.524 -> -0.491, but WNS goes to -1.722: an IO path
  whose D0 cells DPL displaced. That variant needs a DRV pass after DPL.

## 3. Defect 3: data buffers in the clock tree; the g_wbc gated subtree is ~1 ns late [M]
- `flow/tsmc65/pnr/or/cts.tcl` runs `repair_clock_nets` straight after `clock_tree_synthesis`. It warns RSZ-0021 (no
  parasitics: wire-load models) and splits long clock wires with the resizer's buffer list, i.e. data buffers. In r31_B
  cts.odb, 17 data buffers drive clock nets (`cts33.tcl`): wire1-4 (BUFFD16, clk port -> clkbuf_0, 0.6 ns),
  and in gated subtrees wire5/6/10/11/13-19 (BUFFD16; wire13 BUFFD2) and max_length9 / 12 (BUFFD2). None of them exist in place.odb (`ck33.tcl`).
  In PT (routed) the clock network holds 50 non-CK buffers: 16 wire* + 2 max_length* (repair_clock_nets), plus 19 `clkbuf_*` and 13 `rebuffer*`, which
  are the post-CTS setup repair resizing / rebuffering the clock tree (R32 sec. 5.3).
- The worst case is the ICG `clk_gate_g_wbc_hdo_reg_7__6_`. Its leaf net `clknet_2_0_0_u_top_net566817` (4 loads, 0.24 pF)
  drives `max_length12` BUFFD2 (0.68 ns slew out, 0.27 ns) -> wire11 -> wire10 -> 37 g_wbc_* flops. PT tt latency of those
  flops is 2.0-2.79 ns, against p1 / p50 / p99 1.47 / 1.61 / 1.83 ns over the 72 967 register clock pins (`pp33b`, `pp33c`).
- Impact (PT tt, r32_LB, `pp33d.tcl`): all-path WNS -1.684 (`g_wbc_hoy_reg_0_ -> s_wa_3`, launch latency 2.785).
  **Excluding the 37 late flops: -1.349** (next: srd col `s_cJ_q_reg -> s_q[922]`, actout). reg2reg is unaffected
  (-1.027 either way).
- Fix A/B (CTS step replayed from r31_B place.odb with BUFF* / INV* dont_use during repair_clock_nets): 0 data buffers
  on clock nets; repair_clock_nets uses CK cells (max_length12 becomes CKBD4, slew 0.20 vs 0.44 at placement
  parasitics). hoy CP latency 2.261 -> 2.156 ns (OR, placement parasitics). This removes the BUFFD2 part only. The
  rest of the outlier, about +0.8 ns over OR's p50 of 1.39 at placement parasitics, is the subtree's shape: a 3-repeater leaf chain of about 2 mm,
  because the 37 gated flops are spread out. TritonCTS builds every ICG net as its own tree and does not balance them
  against the main tree.

## 4. Checked and not a cause [M]
- PT setup: OCV with no derates, NCCOM 1.0 V 25 C, tcbn65gplustc_ccs, propagated clock, uncertainty 0.25 / 0.05,
  case analysis 336 input pins (EMA / RETN), no ideal nets.
- RC: per-layer R / C of captable_rc.py (tt M2-M7 1.397 ohm/um, 0.222 fF/um; M8 / M9 0.054 ohm/um, 0.345 fF/um; via 1.5 /
  0.22 ohm) are plausible for 65 nm. DEF-summed wirelength per layer equals DR's own report (M8 549 mm, M9 907 mm).
  OR GRT estimate matches PT routed on the limiter (-0.960 vs -1.027). The OR placement estimate uses M3 RC, the same as
  the thin layers where the long nets actually route.
- Global detour: 2-pin nets route at 1.00-1.07x HPWL in every length bin. Detours are local (band corridor).
- QB_TNS 10 vs 100 (sec. 1). Tracks / layer directions / pitches (DEF TRACKS = LEF). No odb obstructions; macros obstruct
  M1-M4 only. PDN coverage M7 5.0 %, M8 14.7 %, M9 4.9 %.

## 5. Observations (not acted on)
- Thick metal is almost unused: M8 + M9 carry 3.0 % of signal wire (1.46 of 48.4 m) and 10.7 % of nets > 1 mm. Long
  nets route on M4-M7 at 1.4 ohm/um, about 26x the M8 / M9 resistance. GRT usage M8 11 % / M9 17 %, M5 37 %, M3 59 %. GRT is
  not timing / resistance aware here, so moving critical long nets to M8 / M9 needs an explicit step.
- GRT counts M7 at 53 % of M5's resources ("Original Resources" 6.94M vs 12.98M) with identical LEF pitch / tracks, and
  the figure stays the same with every M7-M9 PDN wire removed (`gr33.tcl`). It is a tool-internal capacity model,
  not a PDN blockage. It takes about 6M of the 20M horizontal GRT resources.
- PT routed latency p50 1.61 vs L 1.39 (R32 sec. 5.1 still applies). The clk port sits at the north-east corner (3974, 1944):
  0.6 ns of wire repeaters to the root at the die centre.

## 7. What to change (opt-in, not done here)
1. POST_GRT_TCL: `estimate_parasitics -global_routing`, `global_route -start_incremental`, `repair_timing -setup`
   (reg2reg first, with IO false-pathed or repair_tns 100), DPL, `global_route -end_incremental`. Then hold.
2. Place step: after the last incremental gpl, collapse adjacent repeater pairs (coll33.tcl logic), or
   remove_buffers + one repair_design + DRV pass after DPL.
3. CTS step: CK-only cells for repair_clock_nets (dont_use BUFF* / INV* around it, or set the resizer buffer list),
   estimate parasitics first, and look at the g_wbc ICG placement / grouping. dont_touch the clock tree in the post-CTS
   repair (R32).
