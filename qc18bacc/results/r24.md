## FINAL REPORT (R24)
**Verdicts**
- H1 (local density) **confirmed, main cause** [M]: in the divergent pn2 hold legalisation (4 230 k um, setup -1.245 ->
  -12.626) 74.7 % of displacement is in 50 um bins >= 0.9 dense (bins < 0.8: 1.9 %); 61 % is untouched cells pushed by
  16 398 hold buffers. Re-place at incremental gpl density 0.65 (r24_d65) -> every legalisation within 0.121 ns.
- H2 (over macros) **real but secondary** [M]: 5 361 hold cells over P/S/I macros = 21.0 % of displacement (mean 165 um).
  Pre-moving them to the macro edge (QB24_FIX mac) is worse: -17.654 (alone), -5.022 (with H3).
- H3 (hold volume) **partly constraint** [M]: before repair 6 017 endpoints, mostly tiny (reg2reg mean -0.031);
  the setup repair raises them to 17 804 (in2reg 9 731 = 55 %, reg2reg 7 428 = 42 %). in2reg -min delay 0.05P + 1.30
  is below the gated CP latency 1.404 (mean). QB24_HOLD_DMIN 0.10 -> 11 215 endpoints, 10 552 buffers, over-macro
  buffers 5 361 -> 907, legal -3.848: helps, not sufficient. Worst -0.81 = one repair_clock_nets CKBD1 subtree.
- Mechanism: DPL pulls apart chains of BUFFD1 hold buffers (0.46 ns per stage on ~150 fF wires).

**Fix** [R]: QB_GPL_INCR_DENS = QB9_GPL2_DENS = 0.65 (impl/r24/run24.sh pl, QB24_DENS 0.65). r24_d65 (TNS 100):
setup legal -1.358 -> -1.479, hold legal -1.479 -> -1.480, setup1 legal -1.474 -> -1.478, hold1 -1.478 -> -1.478 [S].
r24_d65t (TNS 10, 782 s setup repair, same -1.358): hold legal -1.479 -> -1.593 (0.114) [S]. Cost: pre-repair WNS
-1.761 vs pn2 -1.491 (reg2out htp_reg -> n81318). Also recommended: QB24_HOLD_DMIN 0.10 (H3) and QB_TNS 10 [R].
