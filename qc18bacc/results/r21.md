**Post-CTS repair diverges at util 0.87:** every legalisation of the repaired netlist displaces cells by up to 500 um and costs 3 ns of
setup; a second loop would repeat it.  Production therefore routes the **CTS netlist without post-CTS repair** (`QB_POSTCTS=0`,
restart at the route step from db/cts.odb); timing is reported as found (section 4).  [R] timing repair needs placement headroom
(util <= 0.80) or the RTL / floorplan items of section 2.
**Clock [M] (`impl/r21/clk_lat.tcl` on db/cts.odb, 72 957 clock pins):** latency p10 / p50 / p90 / max = gated (23 960, behind
1 957 ICGs) 1.257 / 1.302 / 1.346 / 1.953 ns, ungated 1.210 / 1.262 / 1.339 / 1.499 ns.  The clk pin is on the north edge of the
west strip (pins9.py): 0.44 ns of port-to-root wire (4 x BUFFD16 from repair_clock_nets) before the H-tree.  **Hub insertion
~1.28 ns, not the 0.90 [E] that R14's top trunk assumed** -> the top must deliver the hub clk ~0.4 ns earlier than the column /
act clocks (R14 bal14.tcl already balances on pin arrival + block insertion).  The IO budget shift is set to this measured
insertion (QB_IO_LAT 1.30; the act-block value 0.77 would have made 25 141 IO endpoints hold-violating, -0.86 ns) and the PT
sign-off uses the same L (R20 lesson: P&R L = sign-off L).

## 4 / 6. Stopped (PI, 11-18 01:50 UTC)
Section 4 (sign-off) was not reached.  The agent was stopped at 500 k context after 8.5 h.  PI killed both production DRs:
r21_a (tau4) was in iteration 3 at 358 k violations after 4.2 h; r21_a2 (tau5) was at 366 k.  Reason: the section 1 budget
already shows the band-450 mid die is infeasible under the budget rule (cuts 106-125 % of capacity at 0.25, limit 75 %).
On top of that, post-CTS repair diverges at util 0.87.  Running DR on a netlist that fails its own budget gate is the
band-aid route.  The fix is the die and floorplan (R22): use the relaxed area (avg > 1.1), move prm north, and make the
budget plus util <= 0.80 a gate before DR.
Kept: the method (budget.sh / cuts21.py / q1), max fanout 32 (-5.2 % std area), the hub clock insertion of 1.28 ns
(QB_IO_LAT 1.30), and the M5 control (B reaches DR iteration 2 at 358 k vs 378 k for uniform 0.25, i.e. -5 %).
