## Prediction (written before the launch)

1. init: `remove_corners default` runs after the 9 scenarios exist (probe on a copy of r68: works, no mode "default"
   exists [M]). Expect NEX-018 = 0 in every step. Low risk.
2. place: unchanged code path; ~20 min [M r68].
3. cts (first qb_io_load with the new act transition): the main code risk. ICC2 must return
   actual_{rise,fall}_transition_{max,min} on act_o ports in every scenario (setup-only scenarios might not give min
   values -> qb5_act_tran error -> step fails). 2 x 9 extra update_timing + ~330k set_input_transition calls: +5-15 min [E].
4. route: redundant via insertion after route_auto and in route_opt's ECO routing (medium effort). Expect the double-via
   rate to rise from 0.38 % to 60-85 % [E]. Risks: longer runtime (+10-20 min [E]); new DRC on the Calibre side (via
   enclosure / density of 2-cut arrays) that ICC2's rules do not flag; a few ps of timing change (lower via R).
5. act slew: act[b] gets the measured act_o[b] transition (r68 ss act_o[8]: 0.218 / 0.141 ns rise / fall vs 0.034 /
   0.024 ns from the bare BUFFD4 [M]) and no driving cell (the BUFFD4 delay was counted twice). act -> reg and act ->
   act_o lose a little from the slower slew but gain the driving-cell delay; net effect on the feedthrough slack
   +/- 30 ps [E]. Most likely new timing failure: max_transition on act nets with long wires at wcl_rcw (input slew
   0.25 ns + wire) - low [E]; hold act -> reg at ml/ff_rcb slightly better (slower slew).
6. eco (DMSA, 9 scenarios): as r68 (1 round). Risk: the fixed-point act transition in the 9 workers adds ~1 min each.
7. post: 9 PT sign-offs + 9 ETMs (was 3). New gates: unannotated pin-to-pin nets (r68: 0 of 6090 not annotated were
   pin-to-pin [M]: 5913 driverless + 177 loadless), MEXT-6. ETM: extract_model_capacitance_limit from the output
   drivers' library max_capacitance (r68 ss: 0.790 pF max [M]). Risk: MEXT-81 on a corner never extracted before
   (wcl, ml, *_rcw / *_rcb) - low.
8. views: create_frame -block_all M7: blockage gate (OBS on M1..M7, none on M8/M9). Risk: write_lef drops the PARTIAL
   antenna data or the M7 PG pins get merged into blockage -> antenna / PG gate fails (medium [E]).
9. calibre: LVS CORRECT 12356 ports expected; DRC: redundant vias are the only geometry change -> watch V*.EN / V*.S
   checks. Antenna 0 expected.
10. Disk: tau5 /tmp at 88 % before the launch [M]; SPEFs are now gzipped (-4.5 GB [E]) and the DMSA NDM copies
    (3.8 GB in r68 [M]) are removed after the eco step. Peak ~89-90 % during eco [E] - watch it.
