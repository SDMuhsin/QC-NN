# R45: unbiased bug hunt (one-line brief), tau6

Three root-cause bugs, each confirmed by a measured fix or minimal repro. Scripts in impl/r45 (opt-in).

1. **Router never sees the kit min-step / end-of-line rules (Calibre G.4, S.5).** The kit LEF 5.6 holds them as LEF57_* properties, which OR loads into a LEF58 min-step type that DRT does not enforce; EOL spacing is dropped (ODB-0388). R38's QB38_RR_MINSTEP duplicated the unenforced type, so it had no effect.
   - Fix: `lef57db45.tcl` writes both rules into the loaded tech before detailed_route. Routing layers only; via layers would add 282 VIA6.R errors.
   - Act block full re-route [M]: Calibre total 57,526 -> 36,229; G.4:M1 21,258 -> 0; S.5 13 -> 0. DR still reaches 0, but takes 3,052 -> 8,841 s at 20 GB.
2. **Column ETM outliers come from extracting with driving cells on the inputs.** `constraints.sdc` sets a BUFFD4 driving cell that `r5a/etm.tcl` keeps during extract_model (MEXT-81 2,433 tt). The production tt ETM has 10 tables more than 2 ns from the median (act[2911] hold-fall up to 7.26 ns). Extracting without driving cells (`etmnodrv45.tcl`) gives 0 outliers and unchanged medians [M].
   - This masked `r7b/etm_fix.py`, `r43/etm43.py` and the R42 -7.2 ns act->col hold.
   - Also affects `hard/pe_etm.tcl`, `r21/pt_hub.tcl` and `r7c/etm_dc.tcl`.
3. **Max-transition/cap never repaired after placement and missing from the sign-off criteria.** On the hub, the ICG move plus CTS raises slew violators 133 -> 269 and cap violators 40 -> 148, 71 of them on ICG enable pins.
   - PT max-transition violators at sign-off: hub tt 14,102 / ss 65,622; act 5,837 / 39,935; column 7,525 / 42,960 [M].
   - Slacks on those pins come from table extrapolation, so they are untrustworthy [E].
   - Fix: violators-only repair_design after CTS, after hold insertion and after GRT; add DRV to the criteria.

Quantified trade-off, not a bug: VIA1.R = one error per wide-pin instance, because lef_pnr.py drops the M1 min-cut rule (the router can't meet it: 20 no-access errors). Counts: act 36,220, hub 85,052 (64 % BUFFD16), column 11,290. Direction [E]: a post-route via-doubling pass.

Open: column ICG CKLNQD1 at 78 fF (max 66) and CKBD16 input slew 0.46 ns, the same issue as R44 ICG sizing. The repro-only M2.A.1 discrepancy is unexplained.
