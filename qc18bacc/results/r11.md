# R11: hierarchical top-level assembly (8 x qb_col_blk + qb_hub + qb_act2_blk + top) through OpenROAD, tau4
[S] tool / script output, [E] estimate, [R] reasoning.  Scripts `impl/r11/` (run.sh nl | hubfp | abs | fp | pnr | sta | dr; probes probe_gpl.tcl, sta_paths.tcl),
tables `results/r11/`.  Netlist r10_rpw (QB_WB_COL=1, R10b), hub partition = R10b's `$B/r10b_ww` (impl/r9b recipe, imported).

## 1. Netlist + equivalence
* **Top netlist [S]** (`impl/r11/top_nl.py`, run.sh nl): R10b's DC partition of r10_rpw (`u_hub` = qb_hub) with
  `u_top_u_act` (qb_act2_ACT_PIPE1) + `u_top_g_dist_0__u_d` (qbt_dreg_W4608) replaced by one black box `u_act_blk`
  (qb_act2_blk); qb_hub / act hierarchy dropped (1 609 of 4 627 modules kept), hub / act / columns = LEF + liberty views.
* **Equivalence [S]** (structural, `equiv.txt`, `equiv_r10b_hub.txt`): flat r10_rpw == hub-partitioned netlist (R10b
  equiv.py: 638 679 / 638 758 inst (+79 split buffers), 702 481 nets with identical terminal sets, 5 185 constant pins
  equal).  Partitioned == top + act black box: 12 707 top instances kept with 0 master / pin-net differences; all
  7 368 block pin bits sit on the net of the chip port bit they replace, s_q through T (`s_q` pin T(k) = hub net
  s_q[k], T(128(4G+a)+32b+8q+i) = 128(4G+b)+32q+8a+i, a permutation: asserted); 0 dead cells left.
* **DC boundary optimisation of the chip's u_act, resolved by tracing (`actmap.txt`) [S]:** `start_k__BAR` = an
  inverted copy of `p_fire[k]` (hub CKND chain) -> block `start` = `u_top_p_fire[1:0]` (no new cell); IN0..IN30 =
  29 buffered copies of rst_n / start (through the hub's reset buffers) + 2 buffered copies of the block's own output
  d_ctx -> dropped (the block has the RTL logic behind its RTL pins); `c_base[8]`, `c_base[17]` have no driver in the
  chip (DC removed them: base[8] only reaches m_ra[8], and m_ra[8:7] are unconnected, map = 128 lines) -> tied 0.
* **Semantic gap, not structural [S from RTL, R]:** R9a's block is `qb_act2 #(ACT_PIPE 2)` (impl/r7c wrapper), the chip
  is `qb_act2 #(1)` + DIST 1 register.  `act` timing is equal, but `done` (`fin_cnt == 3 + ACT_PIPE`, qb_act2.sv:754/760
  -> sequencer start) and the `a_*` descriptors (`a_eot` / `a_ctx` -> qb_chip `stem_eot` -> `img_free`) come one cycle
  later than in r10_rpw.  R7c's "only unused debug a_* shift" is wrong for `done` and `a_eot / a_ctx`.  So top + R9a
  block is NOT cycle-equivalent to r10_rpw.  Fix (no top change): build the act block as qb_act2 #(1) + qbt_dreg #(4608)
  (= the chip's structure, act still straight from a flop), or re-verify the chip with AP 2 / DIST 0.  P&R below is
  unaffected (same pins, ~same area).

* **Mechanism of the placement failures [S + R]:** a probe gpl (`probe_gpl.tcl`, overflow .2, no repair) leaves only
  0.003 mm2 of cells inside block footprints (NW strip .198, north band .299, gap .040 mm2): the cells are not trapped
  in the blocks before repair.  The DRV repair then inserts ~75 k nets' worth of buffers along Steiner routes that cross
  the 3.1-mm array / 1.6-mm hub / act block (no sites there; the hub spans the full die width from y 0), so incremental gpl
  (r11_a) and detailed placement (r11_b, 1 000-um window, every buffer a long diamond search) fail or crawl - the R5b
  mechanism, now with the blocks hard.  Without repair (r11_c) the same placement legalises in seconds.
* **GRT r11_c (`grt_c.txt`, `impl/r11/ovf11.py`) [S]:** 72 345 of 2.66 M gcells (2.72 %) overflowed, overflow V 71 560 /
  H 1 057 tracks:

| region | gcells overflowed | V / H tracks | top source nets |
|---|---|---|---|
| over the act block (780 x 2 420) | 24.3 % | 51 045 / 4 | host data regs g_hpn_q_d, sequencer nets |
| over the array | 1.83 % | 19 859 / 9 | t2 (hub M8 north pins -> column north pins) 17.7 k, host, seq |
| hub-to-act gap | 2.0 % | 621 / 845 | sequencer, s_q, pt |
| hub, NW corner, north | <= 0.03 % | ~0 | |

  Mechanism [R]: the overflow is vertical traffic over hard blocks, where the top has only M8 (V) / M9 (H) above the
  M1-M7 obstructions: (1) the west-strip corridor - the hub's ~2 950 sequencer / glue / host west-strip pins (y 1 601)
  talk to the sequencer, host IOs and act control pins placed north of the act block (y >= 4 711), so ~3 k nets cross the
  2.4-mm act block on M8; (2) t2: 1 056 nets from hub M8 pins up to the column north pins over the 3.1-mm array (R9b
  pin plan).  Overflow > 1 % -> no DR (and DR of the flat chip needed > 30 GB in R8; this host's budget is 10 GB).
* **Clock / timing r11_c [S, OR est.]:** plain CTS latency 1.58-7.55 ns, **skew 5.97 ns**: top H-tree stages are CKBD16
  driving 0.74-1.31 pF of 1-3-mm wires over the blocks (1.38-1.46 ns per stage), gated (ICG) subtrees in the NW
  sequencer get 6-8 ns.  Proposal A (`propa.tcl`): column clk pins moved up the tree (10-19 levels) to the net nearest
  median register arrival (2.83) - 0.96 ns: column clk 2.29-6.10 -> 1.91 (col0) / 2.53 (col7) ns [S].  The hub clk pin
  (R10b pin plan: north edge at x 3 973, under column 7, inaccessible) arrives at 7.71 ns via a long-wire buffer chain.
  Classes after an unlegalised DRV repair (`sta_rt.tcl`, 28.6 k buffers): every class -5.2..-8.0 ns at 1.85 ns
  (hub->col -6.19: launch at the 7.7-ns hub clock; seq->seq -5.55: launch clock 6.27 vs capture 3.1 ns) -> the numbers

  PT tt / ss on routed SPEF: not reached (no DR).

## 5. Blockers (mechanism) and next step
1. **Top floorplan of the west strip (routing and timing) [S + R]:** the act block (780 x 2 420, top = array top) splits
   the strip; the hub's 5 605 west-strip pins (S / map read port 2 653, sequencer / glue / host ~2 950) are at its south
   end, the sequencer's other interfaces at its north end.  Result: 24 % of the gcells over the act block overflow (M8 is
   the only vertical layer above the block's M1-M7), and the sequencer's own paths span 3-5 mm (258 MHz tt ideal clock).
   Fix: act control pins on the act's south edge (R9a QB9_CTL_SIDE bottom), chip IOs (host port) and the sequencer in the
   hub-to-act gap (needs >= ~1.2 mm2: gap 860 x 690 = 0.59 today -> a shorter act block (R9a F2 is 1 970 tall but 900
   wide: needs a >= 910-um west strip in the hub) or a taller gap); only column ctl / cd / weights cross northwards.
2. **t2 over the array [S]:** 1 056 hub M8 north pins -> column north pins, 3.1 mm over the blocks: 17.7 k of the 19.9 k
   overflow records over the array.  Fix: t2 entering the column from the south (column re-pin) or a registered t2
   tap in the north band fed from the hub's west side.
3. **Clock tree [S]:** TritonCTS on this die: skew 5.97 ns (CKBD16 driving 0.74-1.31 pF / 1.4-ns stages over the blocks;
   ICG move made it worse, 7.68); `-insertion_delay` unusable (2 clock nets, no clocked STA path).  The hub clk pin is
   buried under column 7 (R10b pin plan treats clk as a column pin) -> 7.7-8.9 ns hub clock.  Fix: hub clk pin in the
   west strip; a hand-built top clock spine (H-tree in the north band / gap with repeaters every <= 250 um) instead of
   TritonCTS's over-block tree; proposal A applied by tapping (propa.tcl works: column clk 1.56-2.22 vs median 2.43).
4. **DRV repair vs hard blocks [S]:** repair_design puts ~75 k nets' buffers on Steiner routes over the hub / array /
   act (no sites; the hub spans the whole die width from y 0): incremental gpl / DPL fail or run > 1.5 h.  Fix: repair
   with placement blockage awareness (buffer only in the free regions: e.g. pre-route the long top buses as
   M8 / M9 buses with explicit repeater sites in the gap / north band), or fewer long top nets (item 1).
5. **Act block semantics (section 1):** R9a's ACT_PIPE 2 block shifts `done` / `a_eot` / `a_ctx` by one cycle vs
   r10_rpw: rebuild it as qb_act2 #(1) + qbt_dreg, or re-verify the chip at AP 2 / DIST 0.
6. **Area [S]:** 22.85 mm2 (NB 120) needs 468 MHz but does not place; 23.96 mm2 (NB 400) needs 491 MHz > the column's
   489.6.  The std area the top really needs after repair is ~1.0-1.1 mm2 [S], free area at NB 120 is 1.50 mm2.

**Not done:** DR (GRT overflow 2.72 % > 1 %, and GRT alone peaked at 12.2 GB RSS - above my 10-GB budget on tau4,
reported here), routed PT tt / ss, hub / act real abstracts (none exist yet).  **Next step:** items 1 + 3 (west-strip
re-floorplan with act controls south + sequencer / host in an enlarged gap + hub clk pin west), then GRT again; DR only
on a host with >= 30 GB free once overflow < 1 %.
