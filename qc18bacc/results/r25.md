**Verdict on the PI hypothesis** [M, interpretation E]: half right. Local pin / cell density drives the stall: 85 % of
markers are M2/M3/VIA1 pin-access-layer types that track local pin density (rho 0.84-0.94), and macro edges, macro pins,
block pins and the west act strip are NOT the cause (3.8 % near them, below their area share). But it is not a few
hotspots: the rate grows smoothly with density and the markers spread over the whole std-cell area (top 10 % of bins =
59 %). The 177 bins >= 0.8 hold 29 %; capping only them cannot reach convergence [E]. M4/M5 shorts (12 %) behave
differently (rho 0.47-0.59, more macro-adjacent). Global capacity (GRT 0 overflow, R24) is not contradicted. Caveat: per-bin
pin density also tracks local routing demand, so this test cannot fully separate pin access from sub-GCell demand.
