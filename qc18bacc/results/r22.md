## 0. Die and chip A
QB_SB 649 (was 450) -> hub die **3990 x 1950.8 um = 7.784 mm2** [M] (+199.8 um, +0.797 mm2 vs 3990 x 1751 = 6.986 mm2).
mplace10 snaps the die top to the row grid, so QB_SB 650 would give 1952.6 um, which is over the 1951 allowance.
Chip A = 23.80 + 0.797 = **24.60 mm2** [E] (the PI's 24.61 used +200.0 um).  For average TOPS/mm2 > 1.1 this needs
f >= 427 x 24.60 / 24.61 = **~427 MHz (2.34 ns)** [E].  The 2.30 ns target (435 MHz) meets that if it closes.

### 2.1 What each step did [M]
* (a) +200 um of band: the band H cut falls from 108.9 to 84.2 %.  The band height grows x 1.41 but the demand grows x 1.09,
  because the placer spreads.  Item 1 is unchanged at 112 %: the V capacity over S2 / S3 and the crossings are the same.
  The band V cut also stays over 100 %.  prm is the top class of both (41 % and 28 %).
* base: V over the S rows is fine (43 %), but all S-read traffic funnels through the single top band (H 98 %, V 97 % near
  cols 0-2).  Not a candidate.
* (b) pn5: prm leaves the S2 / S3 crossings.  V through S2 / S3 at mid-row drops to 56-65 % (was 112 %).  The band H passes
  (73.2 % on 608 um: demand at x 2225 falls from 5 391 to 4 138 tracks).  The prm band holds 0.34 mm2 of std cells, 0.14 of
  it prm, so its local util is ~76 % (`results/r22/yband22_r22_pn2.txt`).  Three items stay over 75 %:
  (i) the S2 pin edge, 20 um above the S2 bottom.  M4 is at 87 % there and decays to 58 % at mid-row.  The load is S3 / S2
  read traffic plus f_y.
  (ii) the west boundary in the north region.  P0 sits at x 392-831 under the act-out / port pins, and H over the P row
  has only M5 / M7 / M9.
  (iii) band V in col3.
* pn2 (S2 to the south): fixes (iii) (73.1 %) and lowers (i) to 83.3 %.  It is the best GRT so far (0 overflow, 48.1 m,
  WNS -1.18).  But the NW corner gets worse (95.8 %), and band H is 77.0 %.
* pn3 (pn2 + params 0 into the west strip): P1 still covers x 864-1304 at the north, so the hotspot moves to x 1055 north
  (104.2 %).  The P0 / P8 stack (y 1205-1487, x 10-450) narrows the west-strip climb, and s-Q>act-out reroutes over the
  north.  Worse than pn2.

### 2.2 Mechanism of the S-read traffic (the common class of the remaining items) [M]
`impl/r22/sq22.tcl` + `sq22.py` on place.odb (`results/r22/sq22_r22_{a,pn5}.txt`) take the load bbox of every S-macro pin
net, following through repair buffers.
* S Q of columns 0-3 stays local (load centre offset 0 / -1 column, span 126-418 um).
* **S Q of columns 4-7 (4 x 512 nets) all crosses the die middle**: span 1 600-2 000 um, load centre 2-3 columns west.
  This holds in r22_a and r22_pn5 alike.
* S inputs are local (offset 0 / +1).

This is the WB_COL cc / cc+4 S-read pairing that R21 named: the readers of column c+4 sit between it and column c.
Permuting the S-macro slots so that c and c+4 are adjacent would only move the crossing to the writes, which are
column-local with ~283 input nets per macro against 128 Q [E].  So no floorplan-only fix exists for it.
