## Bug 1 (highest): QB39_HUBHALO placement blockage is ignored; cells sit in it
* Evidence [M]: pre_place14.tcl makes the halo with odb::dbBlockage_create (hard, 740-1600 x 1950.8-2010.8 um, present
  in place.odb and cts_rt.odb).  1 645 std cells overlap it in place.odb and cts_rt.odb (AOI22 977, N* 245, NR 90, BUFF
  124, CKB 60; 1 458 named u_*), up to y 2012.4.  check_placement passes.  Re-running detailed_placement on place.odb
  (padding 2, -max_displacement 3000) moves 0 cells (total displacement 0.0): this OpenROAD (2.0-12381) DPL and
  check_placement do not enforce dbBlockage.  The macro keep-out works only because floorplan cut_rows removes the rows
  (0 cells within 7 um of any block).  All 6 GRT-0026 "Missing route to pin" cells (u_seq U9865 ...) are in this strip.
* Impact [M]: DR markers in the strip: it2 1 340, it3 435, it4 337 = 38.5 % of the residual (u_top_pt 300, u_top_s_q
  87 sources).  In Y (no halo) the same strip carried 34.6 k of 86 k it1 markers [M], mostly 10-30 um above the
  hub edge; that is where Y's it3 first batch never finished (> 1.5 h [S r39.md]).  The R39 fix for the Y
  root cause was never applied; V improved for other reasons (density 0.35, DPLPAD, wider gap) [E].
* Fix: remove the rows under the strip before gpl instead of a dbBlockage: split / destroy the dbRows that
  overlap the strip (as cut_rows does for the halos), or a FIXED dummy cover cell.  Add a check after the place
  step: 0 cells in every keep-out rectangle, or fail.  Cost: 860 x 60 um of row area, < 1 % of the free area [E].

## Bug 2: block pins off the top-level routing grid (hub, act)
* Evidence [M] (abs4 LEFs at the r39_v origins, top tracks M2/M4/M6 x = 0.1 + 0.2 k, M8 x = 0.1 + 0.8 k):
  hub u_top_s_q 2 048 M6 pins and act s_q 2 048 M6 pins (0.2 x 0.8 um, 0.4-um pitch) are all centred on x = 0.2 k,
  i.e. half a pitch off track; every M6 track in the pin row touches a pin edge.  Hub u_top_g_r3b_t2_s 1 088 M8 pins
  + clk (0.4 x 0.8) are all off the 0.8-um M8 grid, 546 with no track inside the rect.  DRT-0419 is printed for them
  until its 1 000-message limit; all column pins and all other hub / act pins are on track (0 off).
* Impact [M]: u_top_s_q is a source in 87 of the 337 strip markers at it4 and of 607 M6 shorts at it2; pin access
  is off-grid for 5 185 block pins.  It is a side cause, not the main one [E].
* Fix: snap block pin centres to the track grid in the hub / act pin placement (M6 x = 0.1 + 0.2 k, M8 x = 0.1 +
  0.8 k in block coordinates; block origins are multiples of 0.8 um so this carries to the top).  Needs a block
  re-route (abstract / ETM pins move by 0.1-0.3 um); until then, a LEF-vs-track check per block view.
