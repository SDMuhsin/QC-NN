## 1. RTL: s1c copies grouped by S1 region -- measured, **not done** [S]

* Routed path data (r13_c1d PT tt, `pt_blk_tt_r2r20.rpt`): the s1c class is worst (-0.453 @2.0) but the next three independent
  classes are within 0.05-0.07 ns: S2 select `sel2_1_reg_5 -> u_s2 -> act_reg_2177` -0.40 (11-b sel2_1 broadcast over the
  4 608 act registers: CKBD16 fo 32 0.30 pF + wire buffers 0.34 pF + final NR2D1 at 0.69 ns slew), lb shift
  `shq_reg_2 -> u_lb -> lb_reg_3__*` -0.33..-0.38 (3-b shift select broadcast to all 16 k lb bits of a context, 5 BUFFD16 wire
  buffers), S1 `s1_reg_969` -0.40 (also s1c).  Of the 200 worst violating endpoints, 161 are lb_reg (ctx 0 108, ctx 1 53), 22
  S2 act_reg, 17 S1 s1_reg: s1c is the worst path, not the dominant class.
* Mechanism of the s1c class confirmed (`cells_r13_c1r`, pre-mux cells per copy k, 10 surviving copies): every copy's 1 024
  muxes span the whole S1 area, **y sd 450-514 um, x sd 205-215 um** (y 120-1893); the same 10 246 mux cells partitioned into
  10 y bands would have y sd 32-74 um.  So grouping by S1 home row / region (from the generator's lb -> S1 table) would shrink
  each copy's 0.69-ns buffer tree to a ~200-um band (est. -0.3 ns on that class), and it is cycle-neutral (all copies hold
  the same value).
* Not done: the fmax gain is capped by the sel2_1 / shq broadcast classes at **<= 0.05 ns (~+8 MHz tt)**, i.e. the change
  alone does not move the block toward 489.6; it needs the same treatment for sel2_1 (S2) and shq (lb) -- three RTL changes
  + DC (51 min) + P&R, not compatible with a 6-h box that must also converge DR.  Recommendation [R] in section 5.

## 2. Placement: post-CTS re-spread (`impl/r16/respread.tcl`, `run.sh postcts`) [S]

Mechanism addressed (R13): after the last gpl spread (0.65 / 0.71), DRV repair 2 + CTS + hold buffers (+0.16 mm2) are only
legalised in place and gpl never sees the S1-only DPL padding -> S1 AOI22 clusters at util ~0.86, ~1 signal pin / um2.
Fix: on the CTS checkpoint (all clock buffers present), before the post-CTS repair, an incremental timing-driven gpl at
density = utilisation + 0.05 (0.688), with the act edge chains (4 608) **and the clock tree (11 303 CTS buffers / ICGs) FIRM**,
and the S1 padding made visible to gpl by **temporarily swapping 31 506 S1 cells to the next drive strength whose footprint
is >= width + 2 sites** (equivalent-cell swap, swapped back right after gpl; MOAI22 / AOI222 D2 are not equivalent -> left
alone); then DRV repair of the moved nets (lib qc_repair_drv only), S1 DPL padding 1 site, DPL, and the R13 post-CTS repair.

## 3. Routing layers: **M2-M7 kept** (M8 / M9 not given to the block) [S + R]

* Chip context (results/r14.md, R14 floorplan with this 900 x 1970 block at the top of the west strip): the top routes over
  the act block on M8 -- the clock U riser (2 mm M8 over the act block to the north band), the sequencer split trunks
  (q14s_t) and glue -> weights trunks (sequencer / host regs in the gap below the block, weights north); M8 is the only
  vertical top layer over the hard blocks (blocks OBS M1-M7), and the chip PDN drops its M8 straps (3 um / 20 um) on the
  block's M7 PG pins.  M9 is the top's horizontal layer.  Taking M8 inside the block would need a top-level re-plan of those
  trunks and the block PDN on M8 -- not free.
* Block-side need: GRT (adj 0.05) overflow 0 with M7 15.8 %, M5 31.3 % used; the DR markers are M2 / M3 pin access in the S1
  / lb clusters (R13: M2 0.86 M + M3 0.28 M of 1.72 M), not upper-layer capacity.  M8 would add capacity where the block
  already has slack.

## 5. Next [R]

1. **DR convergence needs a netlist change around S1, not more iterations or more spreading.**  The S1 nets (lbm fanout to 7-23-term
   AOI22 sums in 16 act rows) are the short sites even at util 0.65.  Options by expected effect: (a) register the pre-mux output
   per act-row group (lbm copy per S1 home row band: each S1 cell reads a local copy -> lbm nets become row-local; +1 cycle of
   latency on S1 only if not absorbed, so check the sequencer slack first); (b) S1 as a registered-select mux tree with fewer,
   wider terms (fewer AOI22 pins per s1 bit); (c) only then a taller / wider S1 band (die change).  Measure each with GRT +
   one DR iteration 0 (~50 min) before multi-iteration DR.
2. **Fmax** (tt all-path 438): the broadcast-select classes together -- s1c grouped by S1 band (section 1 data: y sd 450 -> ~50
   um), sel2_1 (S2) and shq (lb shift; lb_reg = 154 of the 200 worst tt endpoints, the R13 lb paths start at shq) replicated per band, and tgo registered before the wer
   select (cin2reg -0.283 tt / -1.000 ss); ss worst ival_reg -> dsc_reg_16 is a new class to trace.  Then DC + P&R once.
