## Mechanism
**The band over columns 0-5 lacks horizontal routing capacity summed over all layers (a horizontal bisection deficit).  Long east-west logical
connections (S Q / s-Q>act-out / reg>s-in / prm traffic) plus the logic of a band placed at 0.95 util add up to more H crossings at every vertical cut than the
450-um band has tracks on all layers.**  Evidence:
1. [S/E] At each vertical cut over col0-5 the H crossings are 5 500-6 300 tracks against 4 198 band H tracks on M3+M5+M7+M9 (131-151 %).  Every H layer is
   over 100 % there, M7 / M9 included (q3 table), so this is not pin access or layer assignment.  Pins per gcell do not separate overflowed from clean gcells
   (63-77 %).
2. [S] Overflow follows the cut ratio across 4 placements: 132-157 % -> 18.40 % (drv), 131-151 % -> 15.66 % (wA4), 101-128 % -> 8.80 % (b450),
   86-103 % -> 1.82 % (b650, overflow left only at the cuts still >= 100 %).  The crossing count hardly moves with band height (b650 5 270-6 300 tracks).
   +200 um of band adds 46 % H capacity and removes 97.6 % of the overflow tracks (1.90 M -> 0.046 M).
3. [S] Density alone does not explain it: col6 bins at util 0.95-1.00 are clean (0.05 overflowed) while col0-5 bins at the same util are 0.92 overflowed.
4. [S] The repair (+0.77 mm2, 0.60 mm2 of it buffers, 92 % on trees > 757 um) is a result of the same long connections (44 % of band H demand
   belongs to logical connections >= 1 mm).  It does not shrink with band height (1.849 / 1.852 / 2.010 mm2 at 450 / 650 / 850 um), and tighter DRV limits (0.30 ns / fo 32) add
   +68 k um2 and +2.7 pt overflow.  The repair adds area to the band but is not the cause.
Secondary lever [S]: incremental-gpl density 0.92 / 0.93 (wA4) vs 0.86 / 0.80 at the same area cuts overflow 15.66 -> 8.80 % and band H demand 6.27 -> 5.14 M
(col0 / col1 util 0.98 / 0.97 -> 0.87 / 0.88, west 0.75 -> 0.83, col7 0.74 -> 0.82).  It still leaves cut ratios of 101-128 %; only more H capacity per cut (band height) or fewer
cross-column connections close it.
Limit of the cut metric [S]: b850 overflows at 65-81 % whole-band cut ratio because the demand sits in a 200-300 um y-strip (section 4), so the deficit is
local to where the long H traffic is placed; extra band height helps only up to ~650 um with this placer.
Not done: no DR (overflow >> 1 %).  The demand / capacity numbers are my model calibrated to GRT totals, so treat ratios near 100 % as +-10 %
(col6 at 102 % calibrated shows 1 % GRT overflow).
