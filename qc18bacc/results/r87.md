## Changes (uncommitted; synced to /tmp/ckp908/qc18bacc/r48, md5 of all 3 files equal [M])

   - `qb_hold_cell_min`: the narrowest cell in the qb_hold_cells list, with width and height read from the library;
   - `qb_hold_reserve_cells <globs>`: the leaf cells driven directly by the matching input ports that are registers or
     drive a register input, which means a register within 2 cell levels.
   Each selected cell gets `create_keepout_margin -type soft -outer {0 0 w 0}` (right side only).  In the ICC2 man page,
   a soft keepout stops only the coarse placer; optimisation and legalisation may use the space [S].

3. blocks/col/icc2.cfg: `P(HOLD_RESERVE) {pw[*] pf[*] w_ld}` is added.  The R85 DENSITY_CAP block is removed.
4. Spatial-query audit [S], covering icc2/, common/, signoff/, blocks/ (and floorplan/ place/ route/ cts/):
   - no get_cells -intersect / -within, get_objects_by_location or -touching is used in any measurement or gate;
   - signoff/pt/io_lat.tcl `remove_from_collection -intersect` is an exact collection intersection, not spatial;
   - the floorplan/*.tcl `-region` hits are pin-placement constraints in the old OpenROAD scripts, not queries.
   Nothing needed replacing.

## Failures by cause
1. Port-net noise, not fixable by PT (reason I + P: the driver is a port, and a buffer cannot fix it) [M].
   - s_rd[487] runs from its south pin (x 326, y 0) as one unbuffered port net of 1003 um (977 um on M6) to loads at
     y 493 and y 988.  The same net in r84 was 577 um.  r84's noise victim was a different port net (b2[21], 880 um).
   - [E] Each run's placement decides which long port net is exposed.  This is systematic, not caused by the
     reservation (0.14 % area).  A fix needs a PI decision on port-net buffering in ICC2: an input isolation buffer at
     the pin, or a max net length on port nets.  No such change was made (no-hedge rule).
2. ETMs missing: a consequence of 1, since run.sh post builds ETMs only after all 9 PT corners pass.
