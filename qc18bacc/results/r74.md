## act_o: dont_touch kept, spacing NDR (`P(FEEDTHRU_NDR) 2`, `qb_feedthru_ndr`)

- dont_touch stays on cell and net: removing it from the net lets ICC2 / PT buffer act_o (R59 / R67 history: the
  feedthrough contract is exactly one BUFFD4 per bit in every abutted copy).  dont_touch stops optimisation, not routing:
  ICC2 accepted the NDR on all 4608 dont_touch nets (route.log "4608 nets have non-default rule qb_feedthru") [M].
- NDR (2 x spacing, default width) over shielding: r73 route [M] act_o 1.18 m of 9.30 m wire (p50 266 um, p90 380 um;
  every victim 343-389 um, i.e. the long ones); horizontal M3 / M5 / M7 1.68 + 1.25 + 0.86 = 3.79 m used of 3 x 15 500
  tracks x 380 um = 17.7 m (21 %).  2 x spacing costs at most 2 extra tracks per act_o wire (+2.4 m, 21 -> ~35 %);
  shielding needs 2 PG wires per net plus connections (+2.4 m and 9216 shield nets' vias) for no larger coupling cut.
- r74 route [M]: GR overflow 675 (0.08 % GRCs) after phase 5, route_auto DRC 0 / antenna 0, final check_routes DRC 0.

(enable_ccd_clock_drc_fixing always_on) left it; PrimeTime fix_eco_drc does not change clock networks.  Not crosstalk.

  CKLNQD1-16 dont_use false, valid_purposes "power hold cts optimization"; route.log "ICG reference list" CKLNQD1-16.
  CKLNQD3 (0.178 pF) would fix it.  So the R65 comment in route.tcl ("ICC2 has no alternative lib cells for them") is
  contradicted [M]; why route_opt does not size this ICG is open (CTS primary corner ss_rcw, CTS-103; violation at ss).
