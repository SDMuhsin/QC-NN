# R19: band-partitioned S1 (opt-in QB_ACT_S1P) for the act block qb_act2_blk

Labels: [M] measured by my scripts on tool output, [S] tool-reported, [E] estimate / model, [R] recommendation.
Scripts `impl/r19/`, summaries `results/r19/`.  Build on tau3 `$QC_BUILD/{syn,pnr}/r19_*`.
**Result:** the R18 vertical bisection overflow is gone.  GRT at adjustment 0.25: **0 overflow** (R18: 145 061 tracks, 21.7 %
gcells); worst act-row cut 7 060 / 9 684 V tracks = **72.9 %** (R18 11 179 = 115 %), as estimated (60.8-73.7 %).  RTL bit- and
cycle-exact (10 / 10 val images, mutant caught).  Final RTL (pre-mux kept): DC area +3.7 %, **DR DRC-clean after 23 iterations** (R16: 1.24 M markers,
no convergence); PT tt / ss reg2reg 427.0 / 269.2 MHz (R16 iteration-0 441.6 / 282.1).

## 1. Estimate and gate [E] (`impl/r19/s1cut.py`; `results/r19/estimate.txt`, `estimate_free.txt`)

PI script (`impl/r19/s1cut_pi.py`) re-run: star partial ORs + greedy min-max -> 4 128 S1 crossings, reproduced, **but its band
map is not placeable**: words per band 0 / 4 / 5 / 8 / 8 / 14 / 4 / 8 / 13 / 4 (8 bands empty, band 8 holds 14 words = 7 k lb
flops).  With the area constraint (4 words = 512 lb flops + 256 pre-mux per band):

| model (15 cuts between act rows; tracks) | S1 partials worst | + write bus, S2, selects, R18 residual (clock, other) | worst / 9 684 (0.25) |
|---|---|---|---|
| star (one 32-b net per (reg, source band) to the home row), balanced, best of 3 seeds | 6 944 | 9 263-9 551 | 95-99 % (x1.3 long-net detour: 120 %) **fail** |
| **cascade** (per reg and side, partials OR-ed band by band toward the home row), balanced | 2 720 | 5 635-5 827 | 58-60 % (x1.3: 70-73 %) |
| **cascade, quarter-pure bands (chosen)**: each band's 4 words in one quarter j // 16 | 2 688 | 5 890 | **60.8 %** (x1.3: 73.7 %) |

* Included: lb write bus from the south edge (sdq word j -> band pj[j], rdq word k -> the bands of j = k + 16 q, i.e. wd = {4{rdq}}:
  1.3-2.4 k at the low cuts); pre-mux and s1c_r per band (local); R13 write-enable replicas re-defined per band (wer[b][s]; possible
  without new flops because each band holds one quarter: wer[b][s] = slot s and quarter Q(b)); S2 from the gen_act2_s2m tables
  (x1.12, calibrated on R18's measured S2 crossings); selects 150 / cut; R18 residual (clock + classes outside its top 7) per cut.
* Quarter purity costs ~nothing (58.2 -> 60.8 %), but raises partials from 460 to 842 (more, shorter chains).
* **Gate: 60.8 % (73.7 % with a 1.3 detour factor) <= 85 % -> proceed with the cascade.**  Star fails the gate at balanced loads.

## 2. RTL (opt-in `QB_ACT_S1P`, needs QB_ACT_S1M = 1 + QB_ACT_S2M + QB_ACT_RPL; defaults unchanged)

* `hw/tb/gen_act2_s1p.py` (imports gen_act2_s2m: same 128 row-homed registers, same 1 470 terms / cond1 conditions; checks that
  every term sits in exactly one band partial) -> appended by `hw/tb/gen_act2.py` to `hw/rtl/qb_act2_net.sv` inside
  `ifdef QB_ACT_S1P` (pre-existing part of the file byte-identical).  Band map `hw/tb/qb_act2_s1p_pj.txt` (generated by s1cut.py).
* Modules: `qba2_s1p_b<b>` x 16 (band b: 1 024-b pre-mux with s1c_r[b], partial ORs of its terms, cascade dn / up, final OR +
  s1 registers of home row b), `qba2_s1p` (wrapper, s1 in qba2_s1r order), `qba2_lb_p` (lb with wer per band), `qba2_s1p_bq`
  (band -> quarter of the write mask).  `hw/rtl/qb_act2.sv`: three `ifdef QB_ACT_S1P` switches (S1 instance, wer decode +
  its sim shadow check, lb instance).  Terms per band 72-105, home registers per band 5-12, chain span mean 8.2 bands.
* Pure reassociation: same terms, conditions and register stage; no new registers (DC: 55 489 vs 55 484 regs [S]).
* Mutant `QBA_MUT_S1P`: band 4 drops term G 0 of register 0 (home row 0).

## 5. Conclusions and next [R]
* The structural fix works: S1 bisection demand 8.3 k -> <= 3.5 k per cut, GRT at 0.25 clean, **first DRC-clean DR of the act
  block** (final RTL, 23 iterations).  Placement locality comes from the netlist (no-seed control gives the same GRT).
* Costs (HW constraint): DC cell area +3.7 % (0.031 mm2) but P&R route-input area 1.141 vs 1.171 mm2 (-2.6 %, less repair);
  die unchanged, cycles unchanged (bench intervals identical); fmax tt reg2reg 441.6 -> 427.0 MHz (-3.3 %, now set by the
  full-height cascades), target 489.6 still not met.
* Next: (1) shorten the long cascades: s1c_r[b] select of the pre-mux is fine; the critical path is the 15-hop OR chain ->
  re-optimise pj for min max-span (not only cut load) or add a mid-chain register only where cycle-neutral (it is not: would
  add a cycle) -> rather a balanced OR tree over bands (log depth, same wires); (2) tgo -> sel2/wer cin2reg (-0.484) needs the
  R16 "register tgo" item; (3) run antenna repair + signoff DRC on r19_mc; abstracts (`run.sh abs r19_mc`) for the top.
* tau3: r19_mc (clean route db), r19_ms / r19_r kept; scratch dirs, SPEFs, r19_d db deleted; R18 dirs + r16_r_killed deleted.
