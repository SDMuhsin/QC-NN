## 1. ICG max_cap root cause (r64, ICC2 probes on its labels)
Chain, each link measured on r64 [M] unless tagged:
1. **place_opt merges the clock gates.** `place_opt.flow.merge_clock_gates` is true by default. In r64/place.log the
   statistics read: 227 ICGs, 211 merged, 51 left. So each acc / shd gate now drives 160 sinks (cts.log gate-level CTS,
   "Number of load sinks = 160"); DC built them as about 32-sink gates [S: netlist clk_gate_*_reg_ instances].
2. **CTS sizes each merged gate to zero slack on estimated parasitics.** CTS honours the library limit: the clock
   setting is the 0.6 pF default, but the ss CKLNQD8 Q limit of 0.4707 pF is enforced. Proof: synthesize_clock_trees
   -postroute on r64 reports ss "Worst Cap cost 0.0150", which is exactly 0.4857 - 0.4707. CTS checks DRC on
   global-route clock parasitics: "Clock Net Global Routing" runs before DRC fixing, and the result is
   "Post Final DRC Fixing NetsWithDRC = 0" (cts.log:2112-2114). Area recovery ("AR: resized 47 out of 230") then
   leaves each gate on the smallest size that passes, here CKLNQD8.
3. **Detail clock routing pushes the nets over the limit.** At the cts label (after route_clock), 4 gates exceed the
   ss limit: 0.4857, 0.4829, 0.4736 and 0.4723 against 0.4707 pF. At the eco label 2 remain (0.4738 and 0.4708), and
   PrimeTime reports the same 2.
4. **No later step can repair them.**
   - ICC2 has no alternative lib cells for any CKLNQD*: get_alternative_lib_cells returns empty and function_id is
     empty. For comparison, CKBD4 has function_id a1.0 and a full list of alternatives.
   - The gated nets are clock nets with derived dont_touch.
   - `synthesize_clock_trees -postroute` (detail: 2 drivers attempted; detail_with_signal_routes: worse, 3 nets at
     cost 0.0208) and `route_opt.flow.enable_ccd_clock_drc_fixing always_on` (4 attempted) all end in "Failed subgraph
     evaluation", with 0 changes.
   - PrimeTime does not change clock nets (r64 finding 12).
- Ruled out [M]:
  - The ICGs are not size_only or dont_touch (both false). CKLNQD12 / 16 have the cts and optimization purposes and are
    not dont_use.
  - The ss max_cap is taken: CTS runs ss as its primary corner (CTS-103) and its cost uses 0.4707.
  - The derate cannot change capacitance. The violation exists at the cts label, before signal routing and SI.
- Fix (native, flow/icc2/place.tcl): `place_opt.flow.merge_clock_gates false`. The netlist's clock gating is kept
  as DC built it.

## 2. One dont_use list (D0)
- **Kit search** [M], on tau5 under $QC_KIT/Documentation:
  - Release note RN_TCBN65GPLUS_140B, section IV "Cells with dont touch and/or dont use attributes". It lists the
    D20 / D24 high-drive cells (Note 4, EM concern), the G* ECO cells (Note 1, not for synthesis) and BHD (Note 2).
    **No D0 cell is listed, and no other kit reason against D0 was found**: no EM, min-size or foundry rule.
  - Databook DB_TCBN65GPLUSTC_140B (69,938 text lines): the grep for "dont", "electromig", "not recommend" and
    "should not" finds no D0 rule.
  - In the repo, `*D0` entered pnr.example.tcl with the first OpenROAD stage (commit 0a64cbd), with no stated reason.
    flow/tsmc65/README.md and pnr/README.md mention D0 / D1 only as QoR: weak drivers on real wires under a zero WLM.
  - So the PI decision was applied.
- **Code [S]:**
  - `flow/tsmc65/site/site.tcl` `QC_DONT_USE {DEL* G* LVL* ISO* CKLHQ* OD18*}` is now the only list. The template
    site.example.tcl is updated to match.
  - `QC_PNR_DONT_USE` is removed from site/pnr.tcl and pnr.example.tcl.
  - Readers: DC (syn/dc.tcl, unchanged), ICC2 init.tcl, PT drv_eco.tcl (now uses site.tcl through col_blk.tcl's
    lib.tcl, no longer sources pnr.tcl), OpenROAD or/lib.tcl, qc18bacc route/act_route.tcl, cts/hub_cts_pre.tcl and
    impl/snitch_cluster/pnr/pre_cts.tcl.
  - Effect on DC: DC now also excludes DEL* / G* / LVL* / ISO* / CKLHQ* / OD18*. This is consistent with the release
    note (no ECO cells in synthesis). No re-synthesis is needed for the column.
- r65 re-ran init (the purpose exclusion is stored in the block), then floorplan, then place.
