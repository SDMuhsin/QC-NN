## 1. Floorplan and area
* **Floorplan [S]** (`fp14.py`): array x0 = **900** (R9a act F2 is 900 wide; x0 860 cannot host it), hub proxy widened to
  4 030 (R11 pin plan shifted +40), columns at 900 + 390 c, act 900 x 1 970 at (0, 2 741.6) = TOP of the west strip
  (top = array top, east edge on column 0's act pins), **gap 900 x 1 140 = 1.03 mm2** below it (sequencer / host regs,
  chip IOs on its west edge via impl/r11/pins.tcl QB11_IOGAP, hub clk pin, all act south pins), north band NB + 6
  weight rows, descriptors NW.
* **Die [S]: NB 300: 4 030 x 5 906.8 = 23.804 mm2 -> f_tt >= 20.5 x A = 488 MHz** (<= 23.9; column 489.6).
  NB 120 (23.08 mm2), 200 (23.40), 240 (23.56) all end in gpl divergence GPL-0307 at overflow 0.18-0.28 (4 densities /
  overflow settings tried, `pnr_runs.txt`); NB 300 converges (overflow 0.15).  Mechanism [S + R]: the north std-cell
  demand: at NB 300 the NW corner below the descriptors (900 x 300) is at util 0.54 = the target density, band 0.24,
  gap 0.30 (`area_place_r14e.txt`): sequencer / glue cells pulled north by the column ctl / cd / weight interfaces
  fill the NW corner; at NB <= 240 that corner is over the target and Nesterov oscillates.  Cutting A below 23.8 needs
  the descriptors out of the NW corner or the north-pulled sequencer logic in the gap (section 8, items 1-2).

## 2. t2 (params -> column t2) mechanism and fix
* **Mechanism [S]:** the column (r5b_blk, fixed) has its 136 t2 pins / column on the NORTH edge, M2, x 215..365 of the
  column; the hub's t2 pins (R10b pins9.py: "column north pins: north edge under column c") are M8 pins spread over the
  whole column width: |hub x - column pin x| median 127, max 344 um.  Over the array the top has only M8 (V) / M9 (H)
  (column OBS M1-M7): each t2 net = M8 up + an M9 jog up to 344 um, 136 bits converging into a 150-um window
  -> R11's 17.7 k overflow records over the array.  Plus **31 t2 bits** go hub -> DC buffer (u_arr BUFFD8) -> column:
  their hub pins have no column pin on their net, so pins9.py put them in the hub WEST STRIP (M4, x ~700); after
  remove_buffers they are 4-mm hub-west-strip -> column-north nets (r14_h worst hub->col -3.1 ns; route guide: M4 / M2 up
  the gap, M7 east over the act / array, M8 up).
* **Fix (hub-side pin placement, `abs14.py`) [S]:** every hub t2 pin (1 087, incl. the 31 traced through the top
  buffer, QB14_TOPV) on an M8 track at its column pin's x, PDN straps avoided; r14_h / i: consecutive tracks (|dx|
  median 12.7 um) -> GRT still overflowed the array by 1 track per 3-um gcell (3-4 bits per gcell vs ~2.8 after the 0.25
  adjustment); r14_j / k: every other track (QB14_T2SP 2, |dx| median 68, max 580 um, the jog done in the band by the
  receiver buffer's side net, section 3) -> t2 no longer an array overflow source.  Each t2 net = straight 3.1-mm M8
  (169 ohm / 1.07 pF tt) + BUFFD4 receiver in the band; hub spec: t2 output max_capacitance 1.0 pF (>= BUFFD16 drivers).

## 3. DRV repair over hard blocks: mechanism and fix
* **Mechanism (probe on R11 r11_c placement, `probe_drv.tcl`, `drv_sum_r11c.txt`, `drv_geo_r11c.txt`) [S]:** of 78 398
  DRV nets, (a) 64 k are short top nets (HPWL < 50 um) with slew propagated from the sequencer's unbuffered select nets
  (remove_buffers) - local, legal; (b) **1 390 block-only nets**: 1 094 hub -> column t2 (3.1 mm, all terminals block pins:
  no row anywhere along any route) + 296 abutted column b2 -> hub (0.5 um; column ETM b2 output slew 0.52 > 0.39 ns, a
  block-pin property); repair puts their buffers on the pins / Steiner points inside the blocks; (c) **913 top nets whose
  pin bbox spans the act block / array** (sequencer / host regs split between the gap and the NW corner; 3.5 m HPWL ->
  ~4.6 k long-wire buffers at the 757-um repair spacing along Steiner trees over the act block).  (b) + (c) are what DPL
  had to move up to ~1.5 mm (r11_b > 1.5 h diamond search, r11_a gpl failure).  r14_d (new floorplan, no fix (3)):
  still DPL-0036 on 2 long-wire repair buffers > 1 000 um from any row.
* **Fix [S]:** (1) floorplan (section 1); (2) `pre_place14.tcl`: block-only nets (after remove_buffers: 61 733, all
  single-side abutted / straight nets) out of the placement-parasitics repair (no legal site; the M3 estimate RC is wrong
  for an M8-only route; GRT-checked, `drv_post`); (3) `split14.tcl`, after gpl: every net with terminals on two sides
  of a block (G gap / N north / M hub-column seam) gets channel-end repeaters: launch BUFFD16 at the driver side's block
  edge, a BUFFD4 receiver per other side, seam pins on the trunk; the over-block trunk is dont_touch for the
  estimate-based repair, the side nets keep normal repair.  r14_j/k: **1 592 nets split (2 092 buffers)**: 499 gap <-> north,
  1 089 seam -> north (t2 + the hub -> 8-column ctl / rst broadcasts: r14_i had them as unbuffered dont_touch block nets,
  hub->col -5.5 ns), 4 seam-driven others.  Trunk geometry: r14_h/i receiver at the far side's centroid (trunk = L over
  the block + band run on thin metal: receiver slew up to 8.6 ns at GRT); r14_j3 both ends at the driver x (straight
  vertical, slew <= 1.7 ns, but 500 trunks within +-200 um: 12 % of the act gcells overflowed); **r14_k: crossing x
  spread evenly over the act width by driver-x rank** (8-um slot buckets, rows next to the edge).
* **Outcome [S]:** with (2)+(3) runs place in ~7-15 min, DPL window 1 000 um, average displacement 1.7-4.2 um, 0 DPL
  failures except r14_k's first try (3 long-wire buffers of side nets; rerun with a 3-mm window: max move 264 um).
  Repair 11-13 k buffers (R11 24.8 k); std area 0.75-0.78 mm2 after placement (R11 1.02), 0.83 after CTS.  DRV at GRT
  parasitics (`r14_*/drv_post.txt`): t2 cap -0.17 pF worst (1 062 pins over the 1.0-pF spec, j3), trunk receivers slew
  <= 1.31 ns over (j3), 18 k top slew violators (sequencer select / ICG enable nets, not re-repaired after GRT).

## 4. Clock (`cts14.tcl`, `bal14.tcl`, `clk14.tcl`)
* **Structure [S]:** TritonCTS only on leaf nets (`-clk_nets`); the trunk is scripted: chip clk pin (gap west edge) -> R
  (CKBD16) -> {H -> hub clk pin (moved to the hub's west-strip north edge, M8 x 454.8; R10b had it under column 7),
  A -> act clk pin (south edge), gap leaves, U riser (2 mm over the act block)} ; riser -> N0 (above the act top) ->
  spine along the band (repair_clock_nets buffers it in the band) -> 8 column taps (CKBD8 at each column clk pin) +
  region leaves (n0..n3, nw); ICG clock pins on their own leaf per region (gated flops are one ICG + subtree later).
  Balancing on the effective arrival = pin arrival + block insertion (column 0.96 = R8d / ETM, hub 0.90 [E], act 0.55 =
  R9a IO latency) vs top-flop group medians: CKBD2 chains (0.026 ns each, measured) before the early taps, 2 passes at
  placement parasitics, then 2 passes at GRT parasitics inside global_route -start/-end_incremental (+ write_guides).
* **Result [S] (`r14_k/clk14.txt`, j3 the same within 0.01 ns):** after TritonCTS: medians spread 2.1, global effective
  skew 2.3 ns; after the placement passes 0.2 / 1.4 ns, but at GRT parasitics it drifts to 0.6-0.85 / 1.1-1.3 ns (riser /
  spine on M8 / M9, which the M3 estimate cannot see); **after the GRT-parasitic passes: the 11 block clk pins within
  4.150-4.198 ns (0.048 ns), group-median spread 0.079 ns, global effective skew 0.837 ns** (rest = ICG subtree tails).
  Latency ~4.2 ns (R11: skew 5.97 ns).  Cost ~1 150 CKBD2 delay cells.  Hold not repaired (CTS hold WNS -1.7..-2.1 ns).

## 8. Remaining blockers
1. **Sequencer split around the act block [S]:** seq -> seq 239 MHz (r14_k, ideal clock, GRT RC) - its north interfaces (column ctl /
   cd, weight address / data, descriptors) pull ~0.2-0.4 mm2 of sequencer / glue into the band / NW corner, so internal
   paths cross the act block twice through trunks.  Fix: fence the sequencer to the gap (placement region) and register
   the north-bound interface (one pipeline stage = cycles, RTL decision for the PI), or move the north interfaces'
   sources south.  Every top-only class is below the 488 MHz this die needs.
2. **Area [S]:** NB 300 is the smallest band that places (NB 120 / 200 / 240: GPL-0307; NW corner at the target density
   at NB 300): A = 23.80 mm2 -> 488 MHz = the column's 489.6 with 0.3 % margin.  Smaller A needs the descriptors out of
   the NW corner or item 1 (north std demand down).  gpl is also fragile at NB 300 (r14_j diverged at 0.17, j2 / j3 / k
   converge with dens 0.58-0.62).
3. **Hub / act proxies [S]:** classes touching hub / act are budget-limited (proxy liberty budgets overlap: act outputs
   0.5 P + hub act inputs 0.6 P) and the hub insertion 0.90 ns is [E]: need R13's act block abstract and a routed hub.
4. **DR convergence [S]:** 604 k -> 362 k markers after 2 iterations (M2 shorts dominate); ~7 h more [E]; marker
   locations unknown (DR stopped before writing the report).
5. **Not done:** hold repair (CTS hold WNS -1.7..-2.1 ns), post-GRT DRV re-repair (18 k top slew violators, t2 cap 1.06 pF
   vs the 1.0-pF hub spec), PT tt / ss on routed SPEF (no clean DR).  Repair buffers > 1 mm from rows still exist in
   corner cases: r14_k first try DPL-0036 on 3 long-wire buffers (side nets), rerun with a 3-mm DPL window.
