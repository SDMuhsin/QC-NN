## 0. Result
* **Gate 1 FAIL [M].**  Under every placement model, the permuted order makes the worst vertical-cut (H-wiring)
  crossing worse, not >= 15 % better: +37 % to +130 %.  Many classes get worse by more than 10 % (reg>s-in at x 2420
  goes from ~400 to ~2 000-2 400 crossings).
* **Model-independent bound [E].**  The S-read mux outputs of all four pairs must reach the act ports at x < 860.  So
  the S-read crossing count at the first column boundary (x 1250) is 2 048 nets for any order.  In pn2 that cut is
  3 422 band-weighted crossings [M], only 13 % under the worst cut (3 945 at x 1640).  So even if no other class got
  worse, the worst cut could fall by at most ~13 % [E], under the 15 % threshold.
* The V proxy over the S3 pin edge (gate item 1) improves: -10 % (model A) / -31 % (model N).  Band-mid V gets worse:
  +4 % / +13 %.
* No RTL change is needed for the hub function.  The opt-in hooks are written and dry-tested (python only; section 2).
  The top level would need the act-feedthrough chain re-ordered (section 3).

## 1. Feasibility gate (no P&R)

### 1.1 Data and column affinity [M]
`impl/r23/aff23.tcl` dumps the pn2 place.odb: 293 832 instances and 313 667 signal nets with 2+ pins.  Logical column of
the anchors:
* S lane L -> column L % 8 (g_srd_col).
* Hub ports at x >= 860 -> column floor((x - 860) / 390).
* params k -> column k.  From each macro, 3 net hops reach column pins of column k only: 88-98 % of the reached
  pins (`results/r23/aff23_affinity.txt`).  **The P macros must move with their column.**
* B (map) macros reach no column pin within 3 hops.  Map 0-2 reach 1 pin each of cols 0-3 and map 3 one each of cols
  4-7; the map_1_* macros reach none.  The B macros therefore stay.

Two-column logic (`impl/r23/pair23.py`, `results/r23/pair23_merged.txt`).  Std cells are counted within 2 hops of
exactly 2 columns, with nets merged through repair buffers and inverters, and nets <= 64 pins:
* distance 4 (c / c+4): 17 552 cells, split evenly between the pairs 04, 15, 26 and 37 (~4 400 each).
* distance 1 (c / c+1): 3 528 cells (34, 67, 45, 56, 23, 12, 01; 330-620 each).
* distance 2, 3, 5 and 6: 484, 553, 167 and 134 cells.

The c / c+4 pairing is real and dominant.  The c / c+1 pairing is about 20 % of it.  Under 0,4,1,5,2,6,3,7, pair 3 / 4
goes from distance 1 to distance 5, and every other c / c+1 pair goes to distance 2.

The write side is column-local at the net level.  Of the reg>s-in, glue-regs and f_y-reg nets after merging, 94 926
touch <= 1 column (S-macro and column-port terminals) and only 57 touch all 8 columns.  This matches the RTL: g_wbc
fills ha / hde / zb [c] from the f_y / w_y of column c only.

### 1.2 Crossing models [M] (`impl/r23/aff23.py`, `results/r23/aff23_{AR,N}.log`, `aff23_cuts_{AR,N}.txt`)
An order puts logical column order[i] in slot i.  The anchors of column c (S macros of the same row, params, column
ports) take the x of the slot that column moves to.  B macros and the west strip stay.  Std cells are handled in one of
three ways:
* **A**: cells move with the slot they sit under.  A on the identity order is the measured placement (A0).  The model
  is biased towards the identity order, because the cells were placed for it.
* **R**: Jacobi clique-model relaxation, started from A, with 40 iterations and rank-preserving x legalisation (keeps
  the pn2 x density profile).
* **N**: the same relaxation from a neutral start (all free cells at one x, 30 unlegalised steps, then 60 legalised).
  This model has no identity bias, but on the identity order it reads 50 % above A0.  Read R and N relative to their
  own identity runs.

Crossings are counted at each column boundary x = 860 + 390 k, weighted by the share of the net's y span that lies in
the logic band (848-1485).  The V proxy counts nets crossing y = 1504.6 (S3 pin edge, gate item 1), y 1166 (band mid)
and y 664 (S2 pin edge), spread uniformly over each net's x span and summed per slot.

**Exhaustive search, model A (`impl/r23/aff23s.py`, `results/r23/aff23s.txt`).**  The nets reduce to 132 signatures

towards the identity order (section 1.2), so this only shows that no order is a clear win from the placed state.

### 1.3 Analytic S-read model (tensor level, g_srd_col) [E]
Each pair cc (columns cc and cc+4) has 512 Q nets per column (4 rows x 128) and 512 mux-output nets to the act ports,
west of x 860.  The mux is taken to sit at the western member.  S-read nets crossing column boundary k:

| k (boundary x) | 1 (1250) | 2 (1640) | 3 (2030) | 4 (2420) | 5 (2810) | 6 (3200) | 7 (3590) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| identity | 2 048 | 2 048 | 2 048 | 2 048 | 1 536 | 1 024 | 512 |
| 0,4,1,5,2,6,3,7 | **2 048** | 1 536 | 1 536 | 1 024 | 1 024 | 512 | 512 |

The PI's "about half as many in the middle" holds at k 4 (-50 %).  But k 1 is unchanged for any order: the mux outputs
funnel west.  At k 2 the S Q crossings disappear, but the mux outputs grow from 1 024 to 1 536.  pn2 A0 band-weighted
crossings are k 1 3 422, k 2 3 945 and k 3 3 789.  So the best case is a worst cut of ~3 422, -13 %, before any other
class gets worse.  The gate threshold of 15 % cannot be met by this mechanism alone.

## 2. What fixes the column x order (found; opt-in hooks written, defaults unchanged, dry-tested only)
* `impl/r23/cord.py`: `QB_CORD` (unset = 01234567, the default).  It maps logical column -> slot.
* `impl/r23/mplace23.py` = mplace22.py unchanged, plus the permutation.  The S macros of column c (lanes 8 r + c, same
  row) and params c (< 8) take the x of the slot pos(c) object.  B macros, params 8, FC, IMG and y stay.
  * Hooked into `impl/r22/run.sh` through the new opt-in `QB22_MPLACE` (previously forced to mplace22.py).
  * The x order itself is set in `impl/r9b/mplace9.py` (B / S at x0 + 390 c, P row at a 449.8 pitch by index), which
    mplace10, mplace15 and mplace22 inherit.  mplace23 post-processes it rather than changing those files.
* `impl/r23/pins23.py` = pins9.py unchanged, plus:
  * column-south fixed pins shifted by 390 (pos(c) - c);
  * `::QB9_NORTH(c)` renamed to `(pos(c))`, which pins9.tcl places under slot k.
  * Hooked into `impl/r15/run15.sh fp` through the new opt-in `QB15_PINS` (pins9.py was hard-coded).
* Dry test on tau5 (pn2 inputs) [M]:
  * identity output identical to pn2's mplace.tcl and pins9_data.tcl (0 diff lines);
  * 04152637 moves 30 macros (24 S + 6 params; columns 0 and 7 keep their slots) and 15 845 fixed column pins.
* No position-dependent timing scripts: `impl/r9b/sdc.py` budgets by bus class, and the r21 / r22 hooks name no
  column positions.  cuts21.py regions are physical.
* **RTL: none needed for the hub.**  A column's index enters the hub only through connectivity: S lanes, params,
  column pins and g_c_<c>.

## 3. Top level (estimate only; not needed now that the gate failed)
* `impl/r14/fp14.py` line 37 puts g_c_<c> at x0 + 390 c.  It would need slot pos(c), with the same `QB_CORD`, so the
  hub column pins stay under their column.
* `impl/r14/abs14.py` line 74-76 sets the hub proxy pins at X0 + 390 c from g_c_<c>.  It would need pos(c).
* The act feedthrough chain: `hw/tb/qb_colarr.sv` (the synthesis body of the chip array via impl/r5b/core_blk.sv)
  chains act column c -> c + 1 by index.
  * Under the permuted order, column 4 (slot 1) would take its act from column 3 (slot 6): 4 608 nets x ~1.9 mm, with
    no room between the abutted blocks.
  * The chain must follow the physical order.  This is functionally a no-op (act_o = act), but it is a change to the
    chip-level wrapper RTL (qb_colarr.sv) or a netlist rewire (impl/r11/top_nl.py).  **Not done, per the no-RTL rule.**
* cts14 / clk14 key the columns by name, so they need no change.

## 4. Recommendation [R]
* Do not pursue the pure column permutation.  The S-read funnel to the west (k 1) does not depend on the order.  The
  hub's column-local write / glue logic loses more locality than the S Q gains.
* The V item (S3 pin edge, 83.3 % in pn2) is the one item the permutation helps (-10 to -31 % on the V proxy).  If
  the PI accepts the H increase on the other items, that would have to be shown by a P&R gate run with
  `QB22_MPLACE=impl/r23/mplace23.py QB15_PINS=impl/r23/pins23.py QB_CORD=04152637` (ready, ~1 h on tau6).  I expect it
  to fail band H (77 % in pn2, and the models give +31-130 % on the worst H cut).
* For S-read, the lever that model-independently lowers the west-boundary cuts is fewer or narrower mux outputs to the
  west, or act ports closer to the S middle, not the pairing.
